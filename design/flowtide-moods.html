Why painted scenes, not photos

Every scene is drawn live, so it moves, never pixelates on a big screen, costs nothing to license and loads instantly. Students get a wallpaper that feels alive without a single image download.

Contrast that stays readable

Each palette pairs one colour family with an opposite accent: violet with amber, jade with koi orange, pink with periwinkle, midnight green with aurora pink, walnut with bottle green, sunset with teal. The accent marks the ring, the start button and the sticker, so the important parts pop out of the scene.

How it fits the code

Each scene is one draw function that replaces a background in SceneLayer. The effects reuse ParticleCanvas, and the timer, buttons and task list stay exactly as they are.
